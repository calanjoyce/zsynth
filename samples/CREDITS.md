# Starter samples

These samples come from the **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by
Versilian Studios LLC, <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>, released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain). Thank you, Versilian Studios.

For ZSynth each one was converted to 16-bit mono 48 kHz, trimmed to start at
the note and to at most 5 seconds (with a short fade), and normalised. Notes
recorded off C were retuned by resampling, so the tuned samples play a C on
the C keys and the sampler plays in tune across the keyboard.
